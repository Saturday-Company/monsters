- Refer to the project by name, accurately, for example in articles, talks, comparisons and

- Say that your project is "based on Monsters" or "a fork of Monsters", as long as it is clear

- Using the marks, or names confusingly similar to them, in a product name, company name,

## Forks

If you distribute a modified version, rename it and replace the logo and the monster designs
with your own. The Apache-2.0 license gives you every right to the code; the name and the
brand are what you need to change.
